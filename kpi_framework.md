# KPI Framework — Amman Digital Market

Define 5 KPIs for the Amman Digital Market. At least 2 must be time-based and 1 must be cohort-based.

---

## KPI 1

- **Name:** Monthly Revenue
- **Definition:** Total revenue generated per month from completed orders.
- **Formula:** Σ (quantity × unit_price) grouped by month
- **Data Source (tables/columns):** order_items.quantity, products.unit_price, orders.order_date
- **Baseline Value:** January = 5878, February = 4313
- **Interpretation:** Revenue shows fluctuations over time, with a significant drop in February followed by recovery.

---

## KPI 2

- **Name:** Monthly Revenue Growth Rate
- **Definition:** Percentage change in revenue compared to the previous month.
- **Formula:** (Current Month Revenue - Previous Month Revenue) / Previous Month Revenue × 100
- **Data Source (tables/columns):** Derived from monthly revenue
- **Baseline Value:** February = -26.6%, March = +10.4%
- **Interpretation:** Indicates volatility in revenue and helps identify growth or decline periods.

---

## KPI 3

- **Name:** Weekly Order Volume
- **Definition:** Number of completed orders per week.
- **Formula:** Count of unique order_id grouped by week
- **Data Source (tables/columns):** orders.order_id, orders.order_date
- **Baseline Value:** Average ≈ 5–7 orders per week
- **Interpretation:** Shows demand consistency and customer activity trends over time.

---

## KPI 4

- **Name:** Revenue by City
- **Definition:** Total revenue generated by customers from each city.
- **Formula:** Sum of order revenue grouped by city
- **Data Source (tables/columns):** customers.city, order_items.quantity, products.unit_price
- **Baseline Value:** Amman = 31,438, Unknown = 15,109, Irbid = 14,501
- **Interpretation:** Identifies top-performing locations and highlights data quality gaps.

---

## KPI 5

- **Name:** Average Order Value by Category
- **Definition:** Average revenue per order for each product category.
- **Formula:** Total revenue per category / number of orders in that category
- **Data Source (tables/columns):** products.category, order_items.quantity, products.unit_price
- **Baseline Value:** Books ≈ 140.9, Electronics ≈ 104.8, Sports ≈ 55.4
- **Interpretation:** Shows which product categories generate higher-value transactions.
